Play with friends in a room or take on up to 3 bots. Works on phones, tablets, and computers.
How to play and invite friends

For remote games, put this HTML page on a public HTTPS website and share that website link with your friends. One person creates a room and shares its 5-letter code; friends open the same website, enter the code, and join. Keep the host's page open for the whole game. Everyone needs internet access.

Match the discard pile by colour or number. Tap the draw pile if you cannot play, then use your card or pass. Action cards skip, reverse, or make the next player draw. Call UNO when you have one card left!
